# Intervening instructions, 2016–2025, and the attribution of each change

Instrucción 1/2025 says it integrates "toda la normativa e Instrucciones … existentes" (p. 2). Once it is "totalmente aplicable", it leaves six instruments without effect (p. 4): 4/2019, 7/2019, 8/2021, 11/2022, 1/2023 and 2/2024. Instrucción 5/2021 (Protocolo Cero) is not on that list; the 2025 text refers to it as current (pp. 9, 10, 13, 29).

Machine-readable versions: [`intervening_instructions.csv`](../../data/03_institutional_rules/intervening_instructions.csv) (instruments) and [`protocol_comparison.csv`](../../data/03_institutional_rules/protocol_comparison.csv) (34 topics, each with `attribution` and `evidence_grade`).

**Evidence grades used throughout.**
- **A**: primary text read.
- **B**: official government summary (La Moncloa).
- **C**: secondary source quoting the instruction: La Marea (journalism) or DEPOL (police academy).
- **D**: no evidence in the sources available.

### What each intervening instruction set out

**8/2021, persistent aggressors (C):**
- an automated report to court and prosecutor regardless of risk level;
- telling the aggressor that control may extend to his other victims;
- device requests must state that other victims exist;
- in special-relevance cases at alto/extremo, a VPER for his other active victims and contact with those inactivated less than 90 days earlier.

**1/2023, the victim's right to know (B/C):**
- applies at medio with special relevance, alto or extremo;
- four named indicators must be present, together with grave violence;
- prior complaints by other victims within five years;
- the only wording allowed is that the aggressor's data "constan" in VioGén;
- the other-victim checks are extended to medio with special relevance.

**2/2024, inactivation reform (B):**
- no-apreciado and bajo cases may be inactivated only without judicial measures, and after at least one in-person interview;
- medio, alto and extremo cases only on death, imprisonment or departure abroad;
- creates "inactivación supervisada" for high-complexity cases.

**2/2024, further details (C):**
- non-location threshold of 6 months;
- renunciation possible only at no apreciado, bajo or medio;
- supervision: 6-month look-back for medio+, special relevance "en algún momento", 6-month minimum, an evaluator-discretion entry criterion, and exit after two VPER-S at no apreciado.

## Attribution of the claimed 2019→2025 changes

**Already in place before 2019:**
- Upward-only override (since at least 2016).
- Personal Safety Plan (since 2016).

**Present in 2019 and continued in 2025:**
- Ex officio assessment without a complaint.
- The special-relevance minimum of 7 days before the first VPER. In 2025 it is narrowed to Medio/Alto, and Extremo may be assessed earlier.
- Later VPER for extremo, alto and medio (<72 h, <7 d, <30 d).
- The mandatory protection floor.
- Escalation when measures exceed capacity.

**Introduced between 2019 and 2025, then consolidated:**

| Change | Introduced by | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| Persistent-aggressor regime | 8/2021, 1/2023 | B/C |
| Resistant cases, in part | 11/2022 | B |
| Supervised cases | 2/2024 | B; details C |
| Renunciation narrowed below alto | 2/2024 | C |
| In-person contact before inactivating bajo | 2/2024 | B |

**Changed twice** (once in 2024, again in 2025):

| Rule | 2019 | 2024 | 2025 | Grade for 2024 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Non-location threshold | 1 month | 6 months | 3 months | C |
| Supervision periods and look-backs | none | 6 months | 3 months | C |
| Contact with the victim before inactivating bajo | at least once | in person | preferably in person | B |

**First documented in 2025.** These may still have earlier origins in texts that are not public.

*Highest-confidence items:*
- Removal of "no apreciado". The 2024 summaries still use the level.
- VPR instead of VPER-C after a new incident (C: DEPOL still mentions VPER-C in 2024).
- The "se procederá" modal for exceptional inactivation (C: 2024 still "podrá").

*Probably 2025:*
- Bajo split by judicial measures (B: Moncloa 2025). DEPOL reports that 2/2024 "modifica la redacción del VPER-S", so an earlier origin of the 60/90-day split cannot be fully excluded.
- First-VPER maximum of 15 days (B: Moncloa 2025 mentions longer periods).

*Other changes:*
- The positive-finding test for ordinary inactivation.
- The never-inactivate rule after a homicide.
- Reactivation for other causes requiring Medio+.
- "No se sabe" answers shown to the court.
- Annex II changes: children block, digital-safety advice, the aggressor-side items conditional on judicial measures.
- Recording of measures made only "recomendable".

**Origin unknown (grade D):**
- 72-hour limit for the consolidated VPR.
- VPER sent to court only when the level rises.
- Device request at Medio limited to special-relevance cases.

## How to close the gaps

Request the full texts of 8/2021, 11/2022, 1/2023 and 2/2024 through the Portal de Transparencia (Ley 19/2013), or from the Secretaría de Estado de Seguridad. Then re-grade the C and D rows in [`protocol_comparison.csv`](../../data/03_institutional_rules/protocol_comparison.csv). The tests require evidence text for every B or C row, so a re-grade must update the evidence too.
