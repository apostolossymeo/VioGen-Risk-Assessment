# Interview prompts for checking practice (specialised police units, prosecutors, victim-support offices)

Each prompt names the rule it tests. The texts cannot answer any of these questions. Never ask an interviewee about an identifiable case.

1. **Override** (2025 §3.8, §6.2). When do officers raise the automatic level, and roughly how often? Is a reason recorded anywhere, even though no duty is stated?
2. **Input coding** (Protocolo Cero §2.3; 2025 §3.6). When information is missing, how do officers choose between marking an indicator "No" and marking it "No se sabe"? Has showing "NS" answers to the court (§3.9) changed that choice?
3. **Bajo without judicial measures** (2025 §4.3–4.4, §6.1). When is the first VPER actually done for these cases, given that no deadline is written? Does VioGén-2 schedule it?
4. **Supervised cases** (2025 §7.4). What happens between the VPER on entry and the exit VPER? Is there a periodic interval? Who decides on an extension?
5. **Ordinary inactivation** (2025 §7.1). How is the "de manera fehaciente" finding documented? Is the court told when a Bajo case is inactivated?
6. **Non-location and renunciation** (2025 §7.2). Did the change from 6 months (2024) to 3 months (2025) change practice? What does "indagar sobre las causas" involve when a victim renounces protection?
7. **Measures actually applied** (Annex II header). Are the measures taken recorded in the Módulo de Actuaciones Policiales? What share of cases have entries?
8. **Contact modality** (Annex II p. 37, Bajo). How often does "en función de los medios disponibles" mean telephone contact instead of an in-person visit?
9. **Electronic devices at Medio** (Annex II). Since the request became limited to special-relevance cases, do units still ask for devices in other Medio cases, under "cualquier otra" measure?
10. **Capacity** (2025 §5.7). How often are capacity shortfalls recorded, and what happens after one is recorded?
11. **The November 2024 break** (Module 1). Did anything change centrally at that point, such as the software, the forms, re-assessment practice or how statistics are compiled? This would be before Instrucción 1/2025 took effect.
